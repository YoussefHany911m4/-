About & services

Youssef Hany helps visitors enquire about listed luxury and exotic cars, discuss selling a car, or request help finding a specific model.

Process

  1. Choose a car from the current inventory.
  2. Ask Youssef about the vehicle and its availability.
  3. Agree the terms directly before proceeding.
  4. Complete the transaction directly with the parties involved.

Commission

The commission terms are being confirmed with the owner. Ask for the full terms before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does this website take payment?

No. This is an inventory showcase and contact website. Purchases and payments do not happen on this website.

Are listed prices guaranteed?

No. Prices and availability can change. Confirm with Youssef before you decide.

Are inspections or warranties included?

This website does not make claims about inspections, warranties or financing. Ask about any terms directly before proceeding.
